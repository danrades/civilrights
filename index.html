Intercultural Studies · Year 10

Freedom, Citizenship and Civil Rights

A source-based learning course on emancipation, Reconstruction, segregation and the Civil Rights Movement.

Content note: This course addresses slavery, racism, segregation, discrimination and political violence. Analyse all materials critically and respectfully.

Guiding question

How did formerly enslaved people move from legal emancipation towards citizenship, equality and civil rights?

Modules

Module 1

Emancipation Proclamation

What did Lincoln declare, and what did he not declare?

 Module 2

Reconstruction

What did lived freedom require after slavery?

 Module 3

Plessy v. Ferguson

Why was “separate but equal” not equal in practice?

 Module 4

Martin Luther King Jr.

How did King present freedom as an unfinished promise?

Source-work method

  1. Identify: Who created the source? When? What type is it?
  2. Understand: What does the source say in simple words?
  3. Contextualise: What was happening at the time?
  4. Analyse: What is the purpose and perspective?
  5. Evaluate: What does it show well? What does it leave out?
