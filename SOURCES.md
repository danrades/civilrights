# Sources and image-rights log

Use this file to document every external historic source and every image used in the course.

## Why this matters

- A historical document may be public domain, while a scan or photograph on a website can have separate terms of use.
- A Library of Congress item may be listed as “No known restrictions on publication”; record the exact item URL and rights advisory.
- A National Park Service page may contain an image with a specific credit line; check that individual image’s reuse terms.
- Do not rely on a search result thumbnail as the final source. Download or link from the owning institution’s item page.

## Citation format in the HTML

Use a caption like:

> Source: U.S. National Archives, *Emancipation Proclamation*, 1 January 1863. Collection URL: [link]. Accessed [date].

Or:

> Source: Library of Congress, *Civil rights march on Washington, D.C.*, 28 August 1963. Rights Advisory: No known restrictions on publication. Collection URL: [link]. Accessed [date].
