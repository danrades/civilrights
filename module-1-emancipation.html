Source A: simplified classroom extract

On 1 January 1863, President Abraham Lincoln declared:

“All persons held as slaves” in the states or parts of states that were rebelling against the United States “shall be … forever free.”

The government and the army of the United States would recognise and protect this freedom. Lincoln also stated that African American men could join the armed forces of the United States.

This is a simplified classroom extract based on the original Emancipation Proclamation.
