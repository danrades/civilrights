How to work with sources

  1. Identify: Who created it? When? What type is it?
  2. Understand: What does it say in simple words?
  3. Contextualise: What was happening at the time?
  4. Analyse: What is its purpose and perspective?
  5. Evaluate: What does it show well? What is missing?
